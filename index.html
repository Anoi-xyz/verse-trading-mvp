What this is: a pipeline that discovers candidate trading strategies across multiple crypto assets, tests each one against real market history, and — critically — validates every result on data it hasn't seen before it's trusted. Most strategies that look profitable in a backtest are actually curve-fit to noise; this project is built to catch that before it costs anything, not after.

What it found: across 25 asset/strategy combinations tested on 730 days of real data, zero survived out-of-sample validation — several looked excellent on the data they were tuned to, then reversed on data they hadn't seen. That's the expected, honest result for simple public technical indicators, and the validation framework caught every one of them.

What's real, not simulated: the execution layer runs against live Uniswap v2 pools on Sepolia testnet, with a confirmed on-chain buy and sell — see the transaction links below.
